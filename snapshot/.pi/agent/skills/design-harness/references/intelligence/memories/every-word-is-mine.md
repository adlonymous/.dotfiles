All writing on the site is first-person, owner voice. No AI-written content, no third-person descriptions.

## Policy

**I write every word.**

- No AI-generated content published without heavy editing
- No third-person marketing copy
- No passive voice hiding authorship
- No "Ahmed did X" — always "I did X"

## Why

The site is my voice. If I didn't write it, it doesn't belong here.

Visitors know they're reading my words, not a bot's approximation of my thoughts.

## Examples

**Good:**
> I designed the Marathon theme for high-contrast work.

**Bad:**
> Ahmed Saleh designed the Marathon theme to provide users with a high-contrast working environment.

**Good:**
> The system uses three foundation variables.

**Bad:**
> The system was designed to use three foundation variables.

## Implementation

- All article content is Markdown authored by me
- CMS enforces first-person voice
- Agents helping with writing must preserve my voice
- Editing agents rewrite in first-person
