## Memory

In writing and code, use double-hyphen `--` instead of the em-dash `—` character. The double-hyphen is the owner's preferred punctuation style for parenthetical phrases and breaks in thought.

## Rules

**Typing**: Type `--` (two hyphens, no spaces) where you would use an em-dash.

**No conversion**: Do not auto-convert `--` to `—` in CMS, editor, or build process.

**Spacing**: No spaces around the double-hyphen: `word--word`, not `word -- word`.

**Reading**: Double-hyphen reads naturally as a dash, preserves ASCII simplicity, and evokes monospace/code aesthetic.

## Examples

**Correct**:
- "Design harnesses--a new concept for AI agents--are written in Markdown."
- "The site uses three custom fonts--Inter Display, Shinka, and Kult Luxury."

**Incorrect**:
- "Design harnesses—a new concept for AI agents—are written in Markdown." ❌ (em-dash)
- "Design harnesses -- a new concept -- are written in Markdown." ❌ (spaces around dashes)

## Rationale

The double-hyphen:
- Is typeable on any keyboard without alt-codes
- Looks intentional and slightly technical
- Preserves plain-text simplicity
- Consistent with owner's voice (plain, direct)
