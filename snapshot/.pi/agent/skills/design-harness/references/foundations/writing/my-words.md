## Rule

All text on Saleh Digital is written in the owner's voice. No lorem ipsum, no "example text here," no generic filler. Every word is deliberate.

## First Person

Write as "I," not "we" or "the team." This is a personal site.

Examples:
- "I am a designer-founder..."
- "I built this tool to..."
- "My experiments in..."

## No Placeholders

Draft content stays private (see `memories/drafts-stay-private.md`). Published content is owner-authored, never placeholder.

Bad:
- "Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet..."
- "This is some example text."
- "[TODO: write introduction]"

Good:
- Real introduction, even if brief
- Honest "coming soon" if truly not ready
- No text at all (better than fake text)

## Tone

Plain, direct, confident. No hype, no hedging.

Examples:
- "This works." not "This should work."
- "I built..." not "I tried to build..."
- "Here's how." not "Here's a possible way."
